Frequently Asked Questions

When is the best time to start marketing for school admissions?

For the April session, brand awareness campaigns should start by late November or December. The aggressive lead generation (Performance Ads) should run from January to March to capture parents when they are actively deciding.
Which platform is better: Google Ads or Facebook Ads?

Both serve different purposes. Google Ads captures "High Intent" (parents actively searching for schools). Facebook/Instagram Ads are best for building brand awareness and capturing passive leads by visually showcasing your campus facilities. We use a mix of both.
Can you guarantee a specific number of admissions?

We guarantee high-quality, verified inquiries (Leads) and strong digital visibility. However, the final "Admission Conversion" depends on your fee structure, school infrastructure, and how well your counseling team handles those leads.
Do we need a website for admission marketing?

A professional school website builds trust with parents and acts as the central place where they can learn about your school, see facilities, and submit admission enquiries..
How is digital marketing better than traditional advertising?

Traditional methods like hoardings and newspapers provide no clear tracking Digital marketing allows you to track every click, enquiry, and lead, making it more cost-effective and measurable..
