Employee Code of Conduct

Mandatory Workplace Rules & Regulations for all Vyapar Wallah Staff and Freelancers.

Welcome to the Vyapar Wallah team. To maintain a high-performance, professional, and respectful environment, all employees, interns, and contractors must strictly adhere to the following rules and regulations. Acceptance of employment constitutes an agreement to abide by these policies.

Mandatory Compliance

Failure to comply with these rules may result in immediate disciplinary action, including loss of pay, suspension, or termination of employment without prior notice.

1. Working Hours & Punctuality

  • Standard Timings: The official working hours are from 10:00 AM to 7:00 PM.
  • Punctuality: Employees are expected to log in or arrive at the office on time. Habitual lateness (more than 3 times a month) will result in a half-day salary deduction.
  • Availability: During working hours, employees must be highly responsive to internal team chats, calls, and client communications.

2. Leave & Attendance Policy

  • Planned Leaves: Any planned leave must be informed and approved by the management at least 3 days in advance.
  • Sick Leaves: In case of sudden illness, the employee must inform the manager before 9:30 AM on the same day.
  • Uninformed Absences: Taking a leave without prior intimation (Absconding) is a serious offense and will result in a direct Loss of Pay (LOP) for 2 days per uninformed day off.

3. Confidentiality & Non-Disclosure (NDA)

As a digital marketing agency, we handle sensitive client data, strategies, and ad accounts. This is the most critical policy of the company.

  • No Data Sharing: Employees are strictly prohibited from sharing client names, contact details, ad strategies, or financial data with anyone outside the agency.
  • No Freelancing with Active Clients: Approaching Vyapar Wallah’s clients for personal freelance work is strictly forbidden and will lead to immediate termination and legal action.
  • Data Security: Do not download or transfer company data, graphic assets, or source codes to personal devices without authorization.

4. Professionalism & Code of Conduct

  • Client Communication: Always maintain a polite, respectful, and professional tone when speaking to clients. Never argue or use inappropriate language.
  • Workplace Decorum: Maintain a positive environment. Office politics, bullying, harassment, or discrimination of any kind will not be tolerated.
  • Deadlines: Marketing relies on timing. Missing campaign launch deadlines or project delivery dates without a valid technical reason is unacceptable.

5. Use of Company Resources

  • Company Property: Laptops, software licenses, and internet provided by the company are strictly for official work.
  • Personal Use: Avoid excessive use of social media for personal entertainment during work hours. (Social media should only be used for marketing research and campaign execution).

6. Resignation & Notice Period

  • Mandatory Notice Period: If an employee wishes to resign, they must serve a mandatory notice period of 30 Days.
  • Handover: During the notice period, the employee must properly hand over all ongoing projects, passwords, and client details to their replacement.
  • Immediate Exit: If an employee leaves without serving the notice period, their full and final settlement (including pending salary) will be put on hold/forfeited.

By working with Vyapar Wallah, you agree to uphold these standards and help us build Bihar's most trusted digital ecosystem.
