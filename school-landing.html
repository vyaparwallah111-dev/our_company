Common Doubts (FAQs)

Aapke dimaag me chal rahe kuch sawaalon ke clear jawaab.

1. Akhbar me ad dena behtar hai ya Facebook par Ads chalana?

Newspaper (Akhbar) ads me pata nahi chalta ki kitne logo ne dekha aur wahan se kitne admission hue. Facebook/Instagram Ads 100% trackable hote hain. Hum specifically unhi parents ko ad dikhate hain jinke bacche school-going age ke hain, jisse aapka budget waste nahi hota aur direct WhatsApp par leads aati hain.

2. Minimum kitna budget chahiye ads run karne ke liye?

Aap bahut chhote budget se start kar sakte hain (e.g. ₹500 - ₹1000 per day ad spend). Jaise-jaise aapko leads aana shuru hongi aur admissions close honge, aap unhi profit ke paiso se apna ad budget scale kar sakte hain.

3. Kya aap log sirf leads laate hain ya admission bhi confirm karate hain?

Hamara primary kaam aapke paas high-quality, verified parents/students ki inquiry (leads) laana hai. Us lead ko final admission me convert karna aapki front-desk/receptionist ka kaam hota hai. Par haan, hum un leads ko WhatsApp automation ke zariye nurture karne me poori madad karte hain.

4. Mera coaching institute ekdum naya hai, kya digital marketing mere liye kaam karegi?

Bilkul! Naye institute ke liye online visibility sabse zyada zaroori hai. Puraane institutes word-of-mouth se chal rahe hain, par aapko naye bachon tak pahunchne ke liye Google Local SEO aur Social Media ki sakht zaroorat padegi taaki log aapko jaan sakein.
